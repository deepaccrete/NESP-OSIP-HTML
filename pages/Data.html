info Source register

OSIP data is aggregated from the Federal Ministry of Power, the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC), the Rural Electrification Agency (REA), NESP partners, and verified private-sector disclosures. Every figure above states its source, historic series state the year they end, and any figure still with FMP and GIZ for validation is marked as such rather than presented as confirmed. Where OSIP covers the regulatory and financial picture, the Nigeria SE4ALL platform carries the matching techno-economic project pipeline.
