Getting Started

Business Registration

Setting up to invest in Nigerian energy runs through three doors, and none of them are closed to foreign investors. Incorporation goes through the Corporate Affairs Commission, registration with the Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission opens the incentive and facilitation channel, and the sector licence follows from whichever regulator covers the business model you have chosen.

domain_add

Incorporate with the CAC

Company incorporation is typically completed within 1 to 7 days. There is no minimum share capital requirement, and 100% foreign ownership is permitted across the energy value chain.

Register with the NIPC

Registration with the Nigerian Investment Promotion Commission is what opens access to fiscal incentives and investment facilitation, including the One-Stop Investment Centre where several federal agencies sit under one roof.

Licence the business model

Generation, distribution, embedded generation and mini-grid activity are licensed by NERC under the Electricity Act 2023. Off-grid and rural electrification programmes are coordinated by the Rural Electrification Agency.

Fiscal Framework

Tax and Fiscal Policy

The headline rates are 30% companies income tax and 7.5% VAT, assessed by the Federal Inland Revenue Service. Sitting on top of them is an incentive stack aimed squarely at renewable energy, most of it administered through the NIPC.

receipt_long

Standard rates

Companies income tax is charged at 30% and VAT at 7.5%. Both apply to energy businesses in the ordinary way, before any incentive is taken into account.

savings

Pioneer Status Incentive

Eligible projects may claim a companies income tax holiday of three to five years, extendable subject to performance conditions. Applications are made through the NIPC.

nipc.gov.ng open_in_new
local_shipping

Equipment reliefs

Eligible renewable energy equipment may attract 0% import duty and VAT exemption under the ECOWAS Common External Tariff and the 2026 NTA exemptions, which matters on capital intensive projects.

Cross-Border Trade

Import and Export

Most energy plant is imported, so customs treatment and product conformity belong in the project plan rather than at the end of it. Clearance is handled by the Nigeria Customs Service and conformity by the Standards Organisation of Nigeria.

payments

Duty and VAT treatment

Eligible renewable energy equipment may attract 0% import duty and VAT exemption under the ECOWAS Common External Tariff and the 2026 NTA exemptions. Confirm the tariff line for your specific equipment before you ship.

verified_user

Conformity: SONCAP and MANCAP

SON administers SONCAP for imported goods and MANCAP for goods manufactured in Nigeria. Imported equipment must meet NIS and IEC standards, with NEMSA certification wherever electrical safety is involved.

son.gov.ng open_in_new
factory

Free zones and export

Projects sited in a free zone operate under the Nigeria Export Processing Zones Authority regime. Export promotion support is handled by the Nigerian Export Promotion Council.

Protections and People

Investment Treaties and Immigration

Two questions come up in every cross-border conversation: what protects the capital, and how do you get your people into the country. Both are answered before a project reaches financial close, not after.

gavel

Statutory investment guarantees

The NIPC Act guarantees against nationalisation or expropriation without due process and compensation, and permits repatriation of capital, profits and dividends through authorised channels.

nipc.gov.ng open_in_new
handshake

Treaties and double taxation

Nigeria is party to bilateral investment treaties and double taxation agreements with a number of partner countries. Whether your home jurisdiction is covered, and on what terms, is worth confirming with the NIPC before you settle the holding structure.

badge

Expatriate quota and CERPAC

Expatriate quota approvals and CERPAC residence permits run through the NIPC One-Stop Investment Centre, where the Federal Ministry of Interior and the Nigeria Immigration Service are represented. Renewable energy applications can be fast-tracked.

Regulatory FAQ

How long does a typical licensing process take?expand_more

Standard NERC generation licence applications typically take 4–6 months from the submission of a complete dossier, including the public hearing phase.

Are there restrictions on foreign equity ownership?expand_more

Under the NIPC Act, 100% foreign ownership is permitted in most energy sectors, provided the company is duly registered with the CAC.

What are the requirements for local content compliance?expand_more

Nigeria mandates specific levels of local workforce and technical service procurement under the NOGICD Act (for O&G) and power sector guidelines.
