About this event

Join policymakers, investors, developers, financiers, and industry leaders to explore opportunities across Nigeria’s rapidly growing clean energy sector.

The forum brings the national pipeline into one room: utility-scale generation, mini-grids, storage, and productive-use projects at every stage from feasibility to financial close. Sessions are structured so a developer with a bankable project and an investor with a mandate can find each other without a broker in between.
