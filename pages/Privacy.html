Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 21 September 2026

This notice explains how the One-Stop Investment Platform (OSIP), operated under the Nigerian Energy Support Programme (NESP) with the Federal Ministry of Power, collects and uses personal data when you use this website. It is written to align with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the rules issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Prototype note: OSIP is currently a demonstration platform. This policy is provided in good faith and will be reviewed and finalised with legal counsel, and registered with the NDPC where required, before the platform handles live personal data at launch.

What data we collect

  • Information you give us — when you register for an account or use the contact form: your name, work email, phone number, organisation, entity type and the content of your enquiry.
  • Information collected automatically — basic analytics about how the site is used (pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location from your IP address). Analytics are only loaded after you give consent (see “Cookies and analytics” below).

How we use it

  • To create and manage your account and saved items.
  • To respond to your enquiries and route them to the right agency or team.
  • To understand and improve how the platform is used.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We rely on your consent, and on our legitimate interest in operating the platform, as the lawful bases for processing under the NDPA.

Cookies and analytics

The platform uses a small number of cookies to make the site work and, with your consent, to measure usage through Google Analytics. No analytics or non-essential cookies are set until you accept them on the consent banner. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing the site’s cookies in your browser.

Sharing

We may share enquiry details with the relevant Ministry, Department or Agency (MDA) so your request can be answered, and with service providers who host or maintain the platform under confidentiality obligations. Some providers (for example, analytics) may process data outside Nigeria; where they do, we take steps consistent with the NDPA’s rules on cross-border transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Contact

Questions about this notice or your data can be sent to info@osip.gov.ng or through the contact form. A monitored data-protection contact and, where required, a Data Protection Officer, will be published before launch.
