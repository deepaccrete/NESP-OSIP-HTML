About Enugu State

Enugu — the historic "Coal City" — is the capital of Enugu State and the administrative heart of Nigeria's South-East. Predominantly rural with substantially rich arable land, over 70% of its people are farmers cultivating crops such as oil palm, cassava, cashew and the renowned Nsukka pepper.

Once known for coal, the state is now pursuing a clean-energy transition — strong solar irradiation, biomass from its large agricultural base, and mini-grid potential for its rural communities — under an aggressive ease-of-doing-business agenda. Through the Enugu State Investment Development Authority (ESIDA), the government offers a one-stop facilitation window, tax incentives and streamlined land access for both domestic and foreign investors.

Anchored by the Akanu Ibiam International Airport, extensive road and rail corridors, and a temperate climate, Enugu presents unique opportunities across renewable energy, agriculture, technology and tourism.
