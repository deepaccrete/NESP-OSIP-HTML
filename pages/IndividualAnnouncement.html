NESP III Results-Based Financing Window 3 opens for mini-grid and solar-hybrid developers

Applications are invited from developers with shovel-ready interconnected and isolated projects in Kaduna, Niger, Plateau and Ogun. Awards are made against verified connections, not capacity installed.

Overview

About this announcement

The Nigerian Energy Support Programme is opening a third results-based financing window for mini-grid and solar-hybrid developers, delivered with the Rural Electrification Agency.

Unlike a capital grant, a results-based award pays against connections that have been verified in the field — meters energised, customers billed, service sustained through a defined observation period. Developers finance construction themselves and are reimbursed per verified connection once the site is operating, which is why the window is open only to projects that already hold their permits and land.

Window 3 gives priority to sites in the four focus states, to clusters that serve productive users alongside households, and to developers who have already delivered under Windows 1 or 2 without an outstanding verification dispute.
