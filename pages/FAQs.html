Group 01

exploreGetting started on OSIP

What is OSIP?expand_more

OSIP is the One-Stop Investment Platform for Nigeria’s clean energy sector: one place to research the priority sectors, read the regulatory framework, browse investment opportunities and reach the agencies behind them. It is delivered under the Nigerian Energy Support Programme (NESP), implemented by Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH in collaboration with the Federal Ministry of Power.

Do I need an account to use the platform?expand_more

Not for most of it. Sectors, Regulations, Data & Insights, News and Events are open to anyone, and so is the public half of every opportunity listing. An account is what unlocks the investor data room on a listing — the commercial figures a promoter is not publishing to the open web.

Which sectors does the platform cover?expand_more

Ten strategic priority sectors, each with its own page: Solar, Bioenergy, Wind, Green Mobility, Clean Cooking, Storage, Small Hydro, Energy Efficiency, Green Hydrogen and Agricultural Productive Use of Energy. Start from the sector index or use the SECTORS menu in the header.

I have never invested in Nigerian energy before. Where should I start?expand_more

In this order: the sector page for whatever you already know something about, then Regulations for the licensing pathway and the agencies that own each step, then Data & Insights for what has actually been deployed and where. When you are ready to be matched to published projects, Register your interest takes you through choosing sectors, registering, and receiving a shortlist built from the profile you gave.

Who is the platform for?expand_more

Investors and financiers looking for a pipeline, developers and promoters looking for capital and for the rules that apply to them, and the public bodies and advisors who need one shared reference. The contact form routes an enquiry as an investment, regulatory, technical support or general question, so you are not writing into a single shared inbox.

Group 02

paymentsInvesting & opportunities

What information does an opportunity listing carry?expand_more

Openly: sector, state, project start and completion dates, promoter or sponsor type, project status, the line ministry responsible, and total project cost. Signed-in members also see the investor data room described below.

What is the “investor data room”, and why is it locked?expand_more

It is the commercial half of a listing — target IRR, payback period, minimum ticket size and lock-up, alongside the extended project detail. Those figures belong to the promoter, so they are shown to signed-in members rather than published openly. Signing in unlocks them on every listing at once; there is no per-project request.

How do I register interest in a specific opportunity?expand_more

Two routes. Register your interest is the guided one: pick the sectors you are interested in, complete registration, and the platform returns a shortlist matched to your institutional profile — the sectors you chose are carried through, so Opportunities opens already filtered. Or write directly through the contact form, choosing “Investment Opportunity” as the subject.

Are there events where I can meet promoters and regulators?expand_more

Yes — OSIP runs investor forums, workshops and roadshows, some on-site and some virtual. Each event page states its dates, venue or meeting link, format and whether registration is open. See News & Events.

Does OSIP invest in, broker or guarantee these projects? Draft answer expand_more

This answer is not yet approved. OSIP presents information supplied by promoters and public agencies. The intended position is that it does not itself invest, act as a broker, or guarantee any return, and that investors carry out their own due diligence — but the exact wording sits with the programme’s legal advisors and this answer must not go live until it comes back signed off.

Group 03

gavelRegulatory & licensing

How long does a typical licensing process take?expand_more

Standard NERC generation licence applications typically take 4–6 months from the submission of a complete dossier, including the public hearing phase.

Are there restrictions on foreign equity ownership?expand_more

Under the NIPC Act, 100% foreign ownership is permitted in most energy sectors, provided the company is duly registered with the CAC.

What are the requirements for local content compliance?expand_more

Nigeria mandates specific levels of local workforce and technical service procurement under the NOGICD Act (for O&G) and power sector guidelines.

Legislation, technical guidelines, fiscal incentives and the agencies behind each are maintained on the Regulations page — including the five-step pathway from registration to financial close.

Group 04

personAccounts & access

How do I create an account?expand_more

Fill in the registration form in one pass — your name, a username, your account role, organisation type, country, contact number and a password — and submit it. The platform emails a six-digit code to the address you gave. Your account is created when you enter that code, not when you submit the form. Until then nothing exists but a pending record. The code is valid for ten minutes.

The verification code has not arrived. What now?expand_more

Check your spam or quarantine folder first, then request a new code. Sending is throttled to one email a minute and five in any half hour for a single address, so a rapid second tap simply does nothing. If you mistyped the address, start the registration again with the correct one — the code went wherever you actually typed.

I received a “someone tried to sign up” notice instead of a code.expand_more

That means an account already exists for the address. Registration answers identically whether an address is free or taken — otherwise the form would be a way to test which addresses are registered here — so the notice goes to the mailbox owner instead. If it was you, sign in as normal or reset your password. If it was not, nothing on your account has changed and no action is needed.

I typed the code wrong too many times and it stopped accepting anything.expand_more

Five incorrect codes discards that pending registration, deliberately — it is what stops a script guessing its way through a six-digit code. Nothing was created and nothing was lost. Start the registration again and a fresh code is sent.

Can I sign in with Google instead?expand_more

Yes. Google passes the platform your email address and display name and nothing else, so the first time you sign in that way you are asked to complete the same profile the email route collects up front — account role, organisation type, country and contact number. Once that is done the account behaves identically to any other.

I registered by email — can I later use “Sign in with Google”?expand_more

Yes, and the reverse too. Both routes resolve to the same account when they carry the same email address, so you will not end up with two profiles. That is only safe because both sides verify the address before it is trusted: Google verifies it before sharing it, and a registration here does not become an account until the emailed code is matched.

I have forgotten my password.expand_more

Use the forgotten-password link on the sign-in screen. A reset link is emailed to your registered address; following it lets you choose a new password and sign in with it straight away, whether the account was originally created by email or through Google.

Can I change my username or profile picture later?expand_more

Both, from your profile. A username is also a sign-in identifier, so it has to be unique across the platform — the form tells you as you type whether the one you want is free. Profile pictures may be JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 2 MB.

Why is there a captcha on sign-in and registration?expand_more

Both are endpoints a script would otherwise hammer — registration sends email to any address the caller names, and sign-in tests passwords. The captcha is the first filter; per-address and per-network limits catch whatever gets past it. In normal use it should be a single click.

Group 05

lockData & privacy

What happens to my details between submitting the form and verifying?expand_more

They are held in a pending registration record and encrypted at rest — the password, but also the contact number, country and organisation, because those are personal data too and there is no reason to leave them legible. The record becomes an account only when the emailed code is matched, so a registration that is never verified never becomes an account.

How is my signed-in session held?expand_more

In an httpOnly session cookie with refresh-token rotation, which means the page’s own JavaScript cannot read your session token — a script injected into a page cannot copy it out. Signing out ends the session.

Can I download the data behind the charts?expand_more

Yes. Data & Insights carries open data downloads in CSV and XLSX — including the state-by-state breakdown of capital deployed — and an OSIP Open Data API for anyone who wants the same figures programmatically.

How do I correct or remove my personal details?expand_more

Your name, role, organisation type, country and contact number are editable from your profile at any time. For anything the profile screen does not cover — including removing an account entirely — write to the OSIP Help Desk at info@osip.gov.ng from the address the account is registered to.
