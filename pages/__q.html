Climate finance

If your capital is climate or transition finance rather than a straight commercial ticket, these are the climate finance sources the OSIP sector pages already name, alongside the national climate change body. OSIP does not administer any of them, and this is not an approved-source list – it is what the sector pages themselves cite. Each one is run by the organisation named on the card, and this page only shows you where to go.

public National Council on Climate Change (NCCC) Nigeria’s national climate change body. Official link being confirmed
eco Global Clean Hydrogen Programme A GEF, UNIDO and ECN initiative supporting hydrogen sector development in Nigeria. Named on the Green Hydrogen page account_balance AfDB Sustainable Energy Fund for Africa (SEFA) Supports clean energy transition projects, including green hydrogen. Named on the Green Hydrogen page savings Green bonds and InfraCredit guarantees Available for well-structured transactions alongside hybrid capital instruments. Named on the Wind and Bioenergy pages co2 Carbon and results-based finance Concessional capital, carbon finance and RBF mechanisms underpin most clean cooking models. Named on the Clean Cooking page handshake Concessional and blended finance Grants, concessional debt and development finance combined with private capital. Named on all ten sector pages

Eligibility, terms and whether a facility is open at all are decided by the organisation running it, not by OSIP. Confirm directly with them before you plan around any of this.
